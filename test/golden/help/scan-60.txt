human-hindsight scan
Find projects and sessions on this machine.

Examples
  human-hindsight scan
    List projects across the tools you use.

  human-hindsight scan --source codex
    Read only Codex history.

Common options
  --source <tool>  History to read: claude, codex, kiro,
                   kirocrew, opencode, or auto (default)
  --follow-up      Offer next steps in a terminal (disable
                   with --no-follow-up)
