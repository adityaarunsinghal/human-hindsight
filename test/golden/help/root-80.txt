Human Hindsight 2.0.0
Turn your coding sessions into reusable prompts and working agreements.

Start here
  npx human-hindsight                    Build or open your library
  npx human-hindsight --dry-run          See your setup and the next step
  npx human-hindsight distill --dry-run  Preview the model plan, send nothing

Everyday commands
  scan     Find projects and sessions on this machine.
  export   Save your messages from coding sessions as Markdown.
  distill  Build a prompt library from your exported sessions.
  gui      Open your library in a browser.
  status   See what has finished and what to run next.

More help
