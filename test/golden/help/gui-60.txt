human-hindsight gui
Open your library in a browser.

Examples
  human-hindsight gui
    Browse prompts, working preferences, and suggested
    subagents.

  human-hindsight gui --tab preferences
    Open directly to your working preferences.

  human-hindsight gui --no-open --port 8765
    Print the address and use a specific port.

Common options
  --tab prompts|preferences|subagents
    Open the prompts, preferences, or subagents overview
  --open
    Open your browser (use --no-open to print the address
    only)
  --port <number>
    Local port (4317 over SSH, otherwise automatic)
