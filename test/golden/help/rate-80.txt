npx human-hindsight rate
Record whether a saved prompt worked for you.

Examples
  npx human-hindsight rate review-changes up
    Give a positive rating. Use a name from list.

  npx human-hindsight rate review-changes down
    Record a negative rating for that prompt.

Common options
  --home <dir>  Data directory (env HUMAN_HINDSIGHT_HOME; legacy
                CC_HINDSIGHT_HOME is also supported)
  --follow-up   Offer next steps in a terminal (disable with --no-follow-up)
