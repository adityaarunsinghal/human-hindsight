npx human-hindsight reflect
Find preferences and reusable prompts in your sessions.
The earlier name distill still works.

Examples
  npx human-hindsight reflect --dry-run
    Preview the plan before sending any session text.

  npx human-hindsight reflect --recent 10
    Start with your ten most recent eligible sessions.

  npx human-hindsight reflect --quality best
    Review the plan using the most capable available models.

Common options
  --dry-run                Print the plan and exit; nothing
                           is sent
  --recent 25              Start with this many of your most
                           recent sessions
  --quality balanced|best  Model plan for reading and
                           writing (default: balanced)
  --project <name>         Only reflect on sessions from
                           this project
