Human Hindsight 2.1.2
Understand how you work. Codify your taste.

Start here
  npx human-hindsight
    Reflect on your history or browse past reflections
  npx human-hindsight --dry-run
    See your setup and the next step
  npx human-hindsight reflect --dry-run
    Preview the reflection plan

Everyday commands
  npx human-hindsight scan     Find projects and sessions on
                               this machine.
  npx human-hindsight export   Save your messages from
                               coding sessions as Markdown.
  npx human-hindsight reflect  Find preferences and reusable
                               prompts in your sessions.
  npx human-hindsight gui      Explore your reflections and
                               library in a browser.
  npx human-hindsight status   See what has finished and
                               what to run next.

More help
  npx human-hindsight help <command>
    Examples and common options
  npx human-hindsight doctor
    Check your setup
