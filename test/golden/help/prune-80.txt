human-hindsight prune
Remove library entries left behind when sessions were regrouped.

Examples
  human-hindsight prune --dry-run
    Preview the entries eligible for removal.

  human-hindsight prune
    Review the list and confirm before anything is removed.

Common options
  --dry-run  List what would be removed; remove nothing
  --force    Also remove entries you edited by hand
