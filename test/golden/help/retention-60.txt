human-hindsight retention
Preview or apply settings that keep local session history
longer.

Examples
  human-hindsight retention
    See the settings available for your installed tools.

  human-hindsight retention --source claude --apply
    Review and confirm changes for Claude Code.

Common options
  --source <tool>  History to read: claude, codex, kiro,
                   kirocrew, opencode, or auto (default)
  --apply          Apply the previewed settings after
                   confirmation
  --dry-run        Preview only, even when --apply is
                   present
