           An answer that fails its checks gets one more try. Up to 10 at a
           time.

           strong-model for choosing and writing roles
           2 Best: strong-model for preference summaries, top-model for choosing
           and writing roles
  Sending  Your working preferences and the evidence quoted for them go to your
           provider through Claude Code (or the CLI the run moves to), the same
           way your normal sessions do, and the calls use your tokens there.
           Before the first model call, two tiny requests, one for each model,
           check that Claude Code is ready.
