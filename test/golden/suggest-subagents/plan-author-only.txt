  Subagent plan

  Reads    Your saved roles: 1 of 2 needs a written prompt (evidence-checker).
  Runs on  Kiro CLI
           This run stays on this CLI.
  Calls    1 call to write a role prompt.
           An answer that fails its checks gets one more try. Up to 10 at a
           time.
  Models   Kiro CLI uses its own default model for every step.
  Sending  The saved roles and their working preferences go to your provider
           through Kiro CLI, the same way your normal sessions do, and the calls
           use your tokens there. Before the first model call, one tiny request
           checks that Kiro CLI is ready.
